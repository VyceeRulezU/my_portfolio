## Problem

Hirenest was a self-initiated concept project exploring a frustration I observed repeatedly in the startup hiring space, growing teams outgrowing spreadsheets but not yet ready for the complexity and cost of enterprise ATS platforms like Greenhouse or Lever.

The brief I set for myself was to design a recruitment tool that felt purpose-built for startups, fast to adopt, easy to navigate, and focused on the two things that matter most in early-stage hiring: tracking where every candidate is in the pipeline and keeping communication from falling through the cracks.

Hiring teams were overwhelmed by disorganized email threads and disjointed applicant tracking spreadsheets.

## Solution

The design was guided by a single constraint, every feature had to earn its place by directly supporting either pipeline clarity or candidate communication. Anything that didn't serve one of those two goals was cut.

**Key design decisions:**

- **Kanban pipeline as the primary view** — applicant tracking was designed around a drag-and-drop kanban board rather than a list or table, giving hiring managers an immediate visual read of where every candidate sits across all open roles without hunting through filters
- **Candidate profiles as the single source of truth:** each candidate profile was designed to consolidate every touchpoint in one place, application materials, interview notes, communication history, and next steps, eliminating the need to cross-reference multiple tools
- **Inline candidate messaging:** communication with candidates was built directly into the candidate profile rather than routed through email, keeping the full conversation thread visible alongside application context at all times
- **Role-based pipeline views:** designed separate views for hiring managers and recruiters, each surfacing the information most relevant to their role without exposing unnecessary complexity
- **Minimal onboarding flow:** the onboarding experience was designed to get a team tracking their first candidate within five minutes of signing up, with sensible pipeline defaults that could be customized later
- **Status-driven notifications:** automated status updates and follow-up reminders were designed into the workflow, reducing the cognitive load on hiring managers to remember where every candidate stands

Built a collaborative recruitment workspace where teams can rank, comment, and communicate with candidates in one unified feed.

## Impact

Reduced the 'Time-to-Hire' metric by an average of 35% in simulated testing cycles.

- **Full end-to-end prototype** delivered in Figma across all core flows — pipeline view, candidate profiles, messaging, and role creation
- **4-month design engagement** covering research, information architecture, wireframing, component design, and interactive prototyping
- Demonstrated a viable design pattern for lightweight ATS tools that serve the underserved startup hiring segment
- Validated the kanban pipeline model as a more intuitive alternative to table-based applicant tracking for small hiring teams
