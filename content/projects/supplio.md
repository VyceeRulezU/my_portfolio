## Overview

Supplio was a self-initiated concept project exploring how design can bring accountability to one of Nigeria's most systemic challenges — a supply chain ecosystem riddled with corruption, manual bottlenecks, and institutional distrust.

As Lead Designer I drove the UX research, information architecture, visual design, and dashboard system, working alongside Gift Okoye to define and execute the full product concept across both the SaaS dashboard and the marketing landing page.

The central design question was straightforward but complex to answer: how do you make a technically heavy system, one involving blockchain validation, compliance automation, and multi-stakeholder reporting, feel simple and actionable for the people who need it most?

Designed an end-to-end dashboard that tracks goods from the manufacturing floor to the final destination, with automated compliance alerts.

Blockchain-powered supply chain management platform tackling global compliance and transparency.

By combining blockchain document validation, real-time shipment tracking, and automated compliance checks, it gives businesses, logistics managers, and regulators a single source of truth.

## Problem

Nigeria's supply chain operates in an environment where structural inefficiencies are deeply embedded across every layer of the process:

- **Corruption and document fraud** — falsified shipment documents and fraudulent compliance certificates are widespread, creating significant financial and legal risk for businesses operating in good faith
- **Manual processing delays** — paper-based document verification and approval workflows introduce days of unnecessary delay at every checkpoint, compounding across the full supply chain lifecycle
- **Zero visibility** — businesses have no real-time view of where their shipments are, what their compliance status is, or where bottlenecks are forming until it's too late to intervene
- **Fragmented stakeholder communication** — businesses, logistics managers, and regulators each operate from separate systems with no shared data layer, making coordination slow, error-prone, and adversarial
- **Regulatory compliance burden** — keeping up with compliance requirements manually is resource-intensive and inconsistent, creating exposure for businesses that can't afford dedicated compliance teams

Supply chains suffered from massive corruption, manual delays, and lack of trust between stakeholders in cross-border trade.

## Solution

The design challenge was twofold: make a technically sophisticated system feel approachable, and serve three fundamentally different user types — businesses, logistics managers, and regulators — without building three separate products.

**Key design decisions:**

- **Blockchain validation as a UX feature, not a technical footnote** — document validation powered by blockchain was surfaced as a visible, trust-building interaction in the UI rather than hidden in the backend, giving users a concrete signal that the documents they're working with are verified and tamper-proof
- **Real-time shipment tracking as the dashboard centrepiece** — live shipment status was designed as the primary entry point into the dashboard, reflecting how logistics managers actually think about their day, giving them immediate situational awareness without hunting through menus
- **Automated compliance checks** — compliance status was surfaced proactively with clear visual indicators rather than requiring users to run manual checks, reducing cognitive load and eliminating missed deadlines
- **Role-based dashboard views** — designed distinct information hierarchies for businesses, logistics managers, and regulators within a unified interface, ensuring each user type sees what's relevant to their role without being overwhelmed by data meant for others
- **Smart reporting layer** — reporting was designed to be generative rather than extractive, surfacing insights and anomalies automatically rather than requiring users to build reports from raw data
- **Landing page design** — a marketing landing page was designed alongside the dashboard to communicate the platform's value proposition to prospective customers across all three user segments

Supplio introduces blockchain document validation and real-time shipment monitoring to create a single source of truth.

## Impact

Concept prototype showcased a potential 40% reduction in document processing time.

Successfully demonstrated how complex, multi-stakeholder enterprise systems can be simplified through disciplined UX research and information architecture
