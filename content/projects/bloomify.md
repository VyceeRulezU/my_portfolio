## Overview

Bloomify was a client project to design and build an e-commerce admin dashboard from the ground up. The client needed a centralized tool that gave their operations team full visibility and control over four core business functions, order management, cart management, invoicing, and ticket servicing, without relying on a bloated third-party platform that didn't fit their specific workflow.

I owned the project end-to-end as both designer and developer, taking it from initial brief through Figma design, front-end build, and live deployment within an 8-week engagement.

Designed for intensive daily use, the interface uses a dense but clear layout to maximize the overview of multi-channel sales.

Order management and operations dashboard for niche e-commerce vendors to scale their business.

## Problem

E-commerce operations at the admin level are often split across multiple disconnected tools — one for orders, another for invoices, a separate helpdesk for tickets, creating a fragmented, inefficient workflow for operations teams:

- **No unified view** — order status, cart abandonment, invoice generation, and support tickets existed in separate systems with no single source of truth, forcing admins to context-switch constantly to get a complete picture of operations
- **Manual invoicing overhead** — generating and tracking invoices manually is time-consuming and error-prone, creating billing delays and disputes that affect cash flow
- **Reactive ticket management** — without a structured ticket servicing workflow built into the same environment as order data, support teams couldn't resolve customer issues efficiently or with full order context
- **Cart visibility gap** — admins had no structured view of cart activity, making it difficult to identify abandonment patterns, intervene on high-value carts, or understand where customers were dropping off
- **Over-reliance on generic platforms** — existing solutions were either too expensive, too complex, or not tailored to the client's specific operational structure

Small e-commerce vendors lacked clear visibility into their inventory health and order fulfillment bottlenecks.

## Solution

The design was built around operational efficiency — every screen, every interaction was evaluated against how quickly an admin could complete their most frequent tasks without unnecessary steps.

**Key design decisions:**

- **Four-module architecture** — the dashboard was structured around four distinct but interconnected modules (Orders, Cart, Invoices, Tickets), each with its own dedicated view and workflow, accessible from a persistent sidebar navigation
- **Orders as the primary entry point** — the main dashboard landing was designed around order management as it represents the highest-frequency admin task, with status indicators, filtering, and quick-action controls surfaced immediately
- **Cart management view** — designed a dedicated cart overview that gives admins visibility into active and abandoned carts, supporting intervention on high-value incomplete purchases
- **Inline invoice generation** — invoicing was integrated directly into the order workflow rather than treated as a separate module, allowing admins to generate and dispatch invoices without leaving the order context
- **Ticket servicing workflow** — support tickets were linked to order data within the same interface, giving support staff full customer and order context when resolving issues, reducing resolution time
- **Vanilla front-end build** — built entirely in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript without a framework, keeping the codebase lightweight, fast-loading, and easy for the client to maintain or extend without framework dependency

Developed an operations dashboard that highlights 'at-risk' orders and automates inventory restock alerts.

- **Delivered in 8 weeks** from design to live deployment
