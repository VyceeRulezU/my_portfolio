## Overview

KoloFund was a collaborative concept project exploring how to make disciplined saving feel accessible and motivating for everyday users in the Nigerian market. The core idea was deceptively simple — use your phone number as your account number, link your bank, and save with intention.

As Lead Designer I drove the visual direction, design system, and end-to-end user experience across both the mobile app and the web landing page, working alongside two other designers on research, ideation, and iteration.

Designed a mobile-first experience focused on simplicity, using vibrant visual cues to represent financial progress.

Clutter-free savings app helping users build lasting financial habits through automation and behavioral UX.

## Problem

Savings behaviour in emerging markets faces a specific set of barriers that most fintech products don't adequately address:

- **Trust deficit** — users are reluctant to link bank accounts to unfamiliar platforms without clear security signals and transparent transaction flows
- **Complexity barrier** — traditional savings products require account numbers, routing details, and manual transfers that create unnecessary friction at the point of intent
- **Lack of structure** — without goal-setting and disciplined saving mechanics, users default to informal saving methods that offer no visibility or accountability
- **Low motivation to maintain habits** — most savings apps treat saving as a transaction rather than a behaviour, with no mechanisms to keep users engaged and on track toward their goals
- **Accessibility gap** — financial products in Nigeria are often designed for the banked and smartphone-literate, leaving a significant portion of the target audience underserved

Young professionals found traditional banking apps too complex and lacked the behavioral nudges needed to maintain consistent savings.

## Solution

The design centered on reducing every possible point of friction between the user's intention to save and the act of saving itself, while building in enough structure to make the habit stick.

**Key design decisions:**

- **Phone number as account identifier** — the single most important UX decision in the product; eliminating the need to remember or manage a separate account number removes a significant cognitive barrier at onboarding and every subsequent transaction
- **Goal-driven saving flows** — savings were designed around named goals rather than generic deposits, giving users a clear purpose for every contribution and a visual progress mechanism to maintain motivation
- **Wallet-first architecture** — the primary saving interaction was designed around the user's existing wallet balance rather than requiring a separate bank transfer, reducing the steps to save to a single deliberate action
- **Seamless withdrawal UX** — equal design attention was given to the withdrawal flow, deliberately avoiding the dark patterns common in savings apps that make withdrawing unnecessarily difficult
- **Trust-forward visual language** — clean, structured UI with clear transaction confirmations, balance visibility, and security indicators throughout, chosen specifically to address the trust deficit common in emerging market fintech
- **Dual platform delivery** — designed both the mobile app experience and a web landing page, ensuring consistent brand language across acquisition and retention touchpoints
- **Interactive prototypes** — both mobile and web prototypes were built in Figma and made publicly available, enabling full flow validation before any development investment

KoloFunds uses gamified milestones and automated 'round-ups' to make saving effortless and mentally rewarding.

## Impact

Concept user testing showed an 85% success rate for maintaining a 3-month saving streak.

- **194 appreciations on Behance** making it one of the strongest performing projects in the portfolio
- **10 saves** by other designers on the platform, signalling strong peer recognition
- **2 comments** with positive engagement from the design community
- Dual prototype delivery validated the complete saving and withdrawal flow across mobile and web
