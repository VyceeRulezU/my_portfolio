## Overview

Osus Properties needed a website that matched the premium positioning of the Dubai real estate market — visually authoritative, fast to navigate, and built to convert. The existing digital presence didn't reflect the quality of their portfolio or the expertise of their team, creating a credibility gap when engaging high-value buyers and investors online.

I was brought in as web designer and developer to deliver the full project end-to-end — from visual design through to live Webflow deployment — within a 6-week engagement.

Built an intuitive investor dashboard that simplifies complex financial data into clear, actionable property performance metrics.

The brief was to build a premium web presence that reflects the agency's market expertise and converts high-intent visitors into qualified leads. Real estate investment platform offering fractional ownership and transparent property tracking.

## Problem

Traditional real estate investment required massive capital and had zero transparency for smaller investors.

Real estate websites in the UAE market face a specific set of challenges:

- **Trust deficit:** high-value property transactions require immediate credibility signals; a generic or poorly designed website actively loses deals before a conversation starts
- **Audience fragmentation:** buyers, sellers, and investors have fundamentally different information needs and entry points; most agency sites treat them as one homogeneous audience
- **Lead leakage;** without clear conversion pathways and strategically placed calls to action, high-intent visitors leave without making contact
- **Market positioning;** Dubai's real estate market is saturated with agencies; without a distinct visual identity and clear value proposition, differentiation is impossible online

## Solution

Developed a fractional ownership platform with real-time portfolio tracking and transparent legal document management.

The design was built around one primary goal, converting a visit into an inquiry, without sacrificing the premium aesthetic that high-value buyers expect from a Dubai property brand.

**Key design decisions:**

- **Premium visual language:** typography, spacing, and imagery were selected to communicate authority and trust, aligning the brand's online presence with the expectations of UAE property investors
- **Audience-segmented navigation:** content and pathways were structured to serve buyers, sellers, and investors distinctly, reducing friction for each group and improving relevance at every entry point
- **Conversion-first layout:** CTAs were placed at high-intent moments throughout the page flow rather than isolated to the footer or contact page, reducing lead leakage
- **Property showcase design:** listings were presented with enough visual weight and detail to communicate value without overwhelming the user or requiring them to leave the page to qualify their interest
- **Webflow development:** built entirely in Webflow for client autonomy post-handoff, allowing the Osus team to update listings, content, and imagery without developer dependency
- **Mobile-optimised:** given UAE's high mobile internet usage among property seekers, the entire experience was designed and tested mobile-first

## Impact

Helped Osus secure their seed round by demonstrating a scalable, high-conversion investor onboarding experience.

- **42% increase in website inquiries** within the first 3 weeks post-launch
- **14 qualified leads generated** through contact forms and property inquiry CTAs.
- **15% improvement in average session duration** versus previous site
- **9 properties listed** and actively showcased through the new platform
- **Delivered in 6 weeks:** on time, from design to live Webflow deployment
