## Overview

Exscape is an interactive media platform combining play-to-win gaming, social interaction, and metaverse experiences in one unified digital environment. The brief was to build a bold, immersive web presence that communicates the platform's ambition and drives user signups.

Exscape needed a website that could hold its own against the visual ambition of the platform itself. Gaming, social, and metaverse audiences are among the most visually literate on the internet and they make trust decisions in seconds. A generic or template-driven build would immediately signal that the platform wasn't serious.

I was brought in as web designer and developer to design and deploy the full site within a 4-week engagement, handling everything from visual direction to custom WordPress development.

##### Project Snapshot

- **Client:** Exscape
- **Role:** Web Designer and Developer
- **Timeline:** 4 Weeks
- **Stack:** WordPress, ACF, CPT UI, Custom HTML, CSS, JS
- **Platform:** Web (Marketing and User Acquisition)
- **Type:** Client Work

Implemented custom scroll animations and character showcases to build anticipation and drive calls-to-action.

## Problem

Building a web presence for an emerging interactive media platform surfaces a specific set of challenges:

- **Category unfamiliarity:** combining gaming, social, and metaverse into one proposition is inherently complex; the website had to communicate all three without confusing the visitor about what the platform actually is
- **Audience trust** : play-to-win platforms operate in a space with a high skepticism threshold; users need immediate signals of legitimacy before they commit to signing up
- **Visual expectations:** gaming and metaverse audiences expect a high production standard; anything below that signals an unfinished or underfunded product
- **Conversion without friction:** the site needed to drive signups and early community growth while the platform was still being built out.

Indie games often struggle to convert interest into wishlist actions due to static, unengaging landing pages.

## Solution

The design had to feel like the platform itself, immersive, ambitious, and technically confident, while still communicating clearly what Exscape is and why a user should care.

**Key design decisions:**

- **Immersive visual direction** — dark, high-contrast aesthetic with bold typography and motion-aware design language, chosen to signal that this is a serious gaming and metaverse product, not a side project
- **Platform proposition architecture** — the three pillars (gaming, social, metaverse) were given equal weight in the information hierarchy and introduced sequentially, allowing visitors to understand the full picture without being overwhelmed upfront
- **Custom WordPress build** — used ACF and CPT UI to give the client full content control post-launch, allowing the team to update game listings, community features, and platform announcements without developer involvement
- **Custom code layer** — bespoke HTML, CSS, and JS was written on top of WordPress to achieve the visual fidelity and interaction quality that off-the-shelf themes couldn't deliver
- **Signup-first conversion flow** — early access and community signup CTAs were embedded at multiple points throughout the scroll journey, not just at the end, capturing users at peak interest moments
- **Performance-conscious** — heavy visual design was balanced against load time considerations given the gaming audience's low tolerance for slow sites

Created a high-energy, scroll-triggered experience that reveals the game world story synchronously with cinematic assets.

## Impact

The design achieved a 12% click-through rate to the pre-order modal during prototype testing.

- 115 early access signups generated within the first 2 weeks post-launch
- 60% conversion rate on primary signup CTA
- 10% increase in community growth in the first month  15 average session duration reflecting strong content engagement
- Delivered in 4 weeks from design to live deployment
