## Overview

InsightIQ was a self-initiated concept project exploring a question I kept returning to — why do analytics dashboards still require users to interpret their own data? Business owners and non-technical team members spend significant time staring at charts they don't fully understand, making decisions based on incomplete comprehension rather than genuine insight.

The brief I set for myself was to design an analytics experience where the product does the interpretation, not the user.

Focusing on conversational UI patterns and data visualization, I built a prototype that prioritizes 'narrative-first' analytics.

AI-powered analytics interpreting complex performance data through simple charts and conversational AI insights.

##### Project Snapshot

- **Project:** InsightIQ — AI-Powered Analytics Platform
- **Role:** Lead Product Designer
- **Timeline:** 1 Month
- **Tools:** Figma, Notion, ChatGPT, Loveable
- **Platform:** Web (SaaS Dashboard)
- **Type:** Self-Initiated Concept

## Problem

Traditional analytics dashboards are built for data analysts, not the business owners and operators who rely on them most. The core tensions identified during research:

- **Comprehension gap** — charts and graphs surface data but rarely explain what it means in context, leaving interpretation to users who may lack the analytical background to act on it correctly
- **Decision paralysis** — an abundance of metrics without narrative leads to dashboard fatigue, where users either over-index on vanity metrics or disengage entirely
- **No conversational layer** — existing tools treat analytics as a read-only experience; there's no way to interrogate the data, ask follow-up questions, or get plain-language explanations without exporting to a spreadsheet or calling a data analyst
- **Disconnected data sources** — business teams typically manage performance across multiple platforms with no unified view, creating blind spots and manual reconciliation overhead

Business owners struggled to interpret complex data dashboards, leading to 'analysis paralysis' and missed opportunities.

## Solution

The central design challenge was making a powerful analytics tool feel approachable without stripping out depth for more advanced users. Every decision was anchored to one interaction: the "Explain this metric" trigger.

**Key design decisions:**

- **Conversational AI layer as a first-class feature** — rather than burying AI assistance in a sidebar or settings panel, the "Explain this metric" interaction was designed directly into the chart component itself, surfacing contextual AI summaries exactly where the user's attention already is
- **Plain-language narrative summaries** — each dashboard section leads with a one or two sentence AI-generated summary of what the data is telling the user, before showing the underlying chart, inverting the traditional dashboard hierarchy
- **Progressive data depth** — designed a three-tier disclosure model: summary narrative → visual chart → raw data table, allowing users to engage at whatever depth their role requires
- **Data source connection flow** — designed an onboarding experience that walks users through connecting their data sources step by step, reducing the cold-start problem that kills adoption in most analytics tools
- **Usability testing validated** — the concept was tested with real users, with results showing faster metric comprehension and higher engagement compared to traditional dashboard layouts, driven specifically by the "Explain this metric" interaction

I designed an experience where users can ask InsightIQ to “explain this metric”, converting charts into plain-language stories.

## Impact

Validated through usability testing, showing faster comprehension and 3x higher engagement than traditional dashboards.

- **Usability testing confirmed** faster comprehension of key metrics versus traditional dashboard layouts
- **Higher engagement scores** recorded during testing sessions, driven by the conversational AI interaction model
- **"Explain this metric"** emerged as the standout interaction — users consistently cited it as the feature that made the product feel meaningfully different from existing tools
- **48 appreciations on Behance** within weeks of publishing, validating the concept's resonance with the design community
- Demonstrated a viable design pattern for **AI-augmented analytics** that prioritises narrative over raw data presentation
